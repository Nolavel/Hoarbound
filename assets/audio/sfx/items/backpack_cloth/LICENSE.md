# Backpack cloth handling

- Title: Cloth Grabbing
- Author: j1987
- Source: https://freesound.org/people/j1987/sounds/237047/
- License: CC0-1.0, https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
- Supplied by the project owner as a cut pack: OGG Vorbis, 48 kHz, mono, nine non-looping clips.
