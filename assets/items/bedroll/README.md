# Bedroll: real prototype and Blender study

The accepted construction reference combines the [OneTigris LIGHTDREAM](https://camping.onetigris.com/products/lightdream-sleeping-pad) and its [photographed deflate → fold lengthwise → roll sequence](https://www.fullcampshop.com/product-page/lightdream-self-inflating-matterss) with the [USGI pad's two attached retention straps](https://www.usamm.com/products/gov-issue-foam-sleeping-pad). OneTigris lists 181 × 64 × 5 cm open and 31 × 17 × 17 cm packed; the USGI pad is 72 × 24 × 3/8 inches. Neither is copied as a product. The [Hoarbound concept sheet](../../../docs/asset_previews/bedroll/bedroll_real_prototype_concept.png) shows how their construction is adapted to the game's 190 × 75 cm pad.

The current Blender study is `source/bedroll_one_sheet.blend`, generated by `source/build_bedroll_one_sheet.py` in Blender 5.2. It keeps **one connected, visible mat mesh** from open through folded and rolled states. A fold bone turns the left half over; shape keys curl the same mesh into the roll. Two separate straps are attached at the finishing edge and wrap around the roll. The mesh narrows from 75 cm to about 37.5 cm; the rolled diameter is about 21 cm. Frame 1 is open, frame 26 folded, frame 42 partly rolled and frame 64 packed. `source/qa_bedroll_one_sheet.py` checks mesh continuity and dimensions. The studio collection and review cameras are for Blender presentation only.

Render with `BEDROLL_OUT=/path/to/output blender -b -t 8 --python source/build_bedroll_one_sheet.py`. The resulting `.blend` can be rendered through `source/render_one_sheet_animation.py`. The [four-state PNG sheet](../../../docs/asset_previews/bedroll/bedroll_one_sheet_review_grid.png), [end view](../../../docs/asset_previews/bedroll/bedroll_one_sheet_rolled_end.png) and [review animation](../../../docs/asset_previews/bedroll/bedroll_one_sheet_animation_review.mp4) show the current result. The animation is a 15-frame review sampling of the 64-frame Blender motion, not a game export.

The earlier `source/bedroll_candidate.blend`, `source/build_bedroll.py` and unsuffixed previews are **rejected history**: that study substituted a cylinder at frame 54. Do not export it to Godot. The new study is still a Blender visual and mechanical candidate; it has not replaced the existing Godot backpack prop or been fitted to Henry's rig.
