# Padded mat Blender study

The current visual references are the owner's unfolded five-section mat and thick rolled bedding photographs. They show different products; this study combines their visible construction into one Hoarbound prop. The unfolded model is 190 × 75 cm with an approximately 32 mm padded body. It rolls **across its full width**, with no lengthwise fold, into a cylinder about 75 cm wide and 33 cm in diameter. The end shows roughly three to four broad turns.

`source/bedroll_one_sheet.blend` is generated with Blender 5.2 by `source/build_bedroll_one_sheet.py`. The mat remains one connected mesh through the 76-frame sequence: open at frame 1, rolling from frame 9, clean roll at frame 64. QA checks that `Bedroll_Continuous_Sheet` retains the same mesh datablock and 16,100 source vertices at sampled frames; the modifier-evaluated mesh remains 25,240 vertices. The MP4 renderer reads this saved `.blend` and changes only the frame and review camera. Two **removable** webbing loops enter only after the roll is complete; they slide into position by frame 70 and compress the padded surface by about 10 mm at frame 76. The straps are separate objects and are not part of the flat mat.

`source/generate_bedroll_textures.py` produces six maps under `textures/`: dark umber and muted burgundy with slate-grey variation on both faces, sparse directed scuffs, abrasion conditioned by edges and seams, a few fabric splits, an indentation mask, and close-range woven relief. Three pressure hollows displace the original mat vertices; QA measures one at 7.4 mm. The stitched channels compress both faces, and two broader creases form in the exterior wrap during rolling. The maps are packed inside the `.blend` and use surface-bound UVs. Regenerate textures before rebuilding the Blender scene if editing the paint source.

Run `BEDROLL_OUT=/path/to/output blender -b -t 8 --python source/build_bedroll_one_sheet.py` from this directory. `source/qa_bedroll_one_sheet.py` verifies one connected mat mesh, packed maps, separated pre/post-strap states and geometry compression. `source/render_one_sheet_animation.py` renders review frames from the saved `.blend`. The current overview is [`bedroll_one_sheet_review_grid.png`](../../../docs/asset_previews/bedroll/bedroll_one_sheet_review_grid.png), with an [MP4 transition](../../../docs/asset_previews/bedroll/bedroll_one_sheet_animation_review.mp4); individual open, side, half-roll, clean-roll, strapped, strap-detail and end PNGs are alongside them.

The earlier `source/bedroll_candidate.blend` substituted a separate cylinder during animation. The following lengthwise-fold study produced too many visible layers and short-roll proportions. Both are rejected; do not export them to Godot. This current scene is a Blender review candidate, not yet fitted to Henry's backpack or integrated into the game. The high-resolution close-up still reads somewhat smooth; the material and soft-body character need art review before production approval.
