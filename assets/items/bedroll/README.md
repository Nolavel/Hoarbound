# Bedroll

The source is a 190 × 75 cm thin sleeping mat, with a roughly 3.5 mm sheet. Its right half folds lengthwise over the left to roughly 37.5 cm wide, then the doubled sheet rolls into an approximately 39.9 cm wide, 13.1 cm diameter roll. The layers compress by 30% while winding, without changing the open mat's size or scaling the packed model separately. This closely matches the previous 40 × 12.4 cm placeholder on Henry's pack. The same connected sheet is present in every Blender frame; its leading sewn hem winds tightly into the centre without a separate end cap or plug. Two removable 25 mm webbing loops appear **after** the sheet is rolled and press 10 mm rounded grooves into the roll geometry, with soft raised shoulders beside them. The centre between straps does not move during cinching. The compact two-slot polymer ladderlocks use the published [ITW Nexus Jet LL 25 dimensions and threading diagram](https://global.itwnexus.com/webfm_send/25/1), rather than oversized square buckles.

`source/bedroll_one_sheet.blend` is the Blender 5.2 source. Frame 1 is laid flat, frames 4–22 show the lengthwise fold, frame 80 is rolled without straps, and frame 92 is strapped. Reversing that sequence opens the same mat. `bedroll_open.glb` and `bedroll_packed.glb` are static snapshots of that source; their corresponding `.scn` files are the actual Godot runtime scenes. `BedrollComponent` instantiates the open scene on the ground. `BackpackModel` mounts the packed scene in its original place across the top, ahead of the handle, with two short tabs leaning toward Henry's back to meet the roll's loops; `PackRig` shows it only while the bedroll is in inventory. The game currently switches between these two states on pickup; the Blender fold and roll animation is an authored review sequence, not a claimed in-game packing animation.

The painterly oxblood, brown and slate maps live in `textures/`. `source/generate_bedroll_textures.py` makes the base colour, wear, fabric splits, indentation and fibre masks. `source/bake_game_albedo.py` combines the paint masks into portable albedo maps; the Godot scenes use the project's NPR material adapter. `source/bedroll_candidate.blend` is the earlier rejected cylinder-substitution study and must not be used for export.

To rebuild, run the texture generator, `bake_game_albedo.py`, then `build_bedroll_one_sheet.py` with `BEDROLL_OUT` set to an output directory. Run `qa_bedroll_one_sheet.py` against that `.blend`, `export_bedroll_game_ready.py` for the two GLBs, and `export_godot_scenes.gd` in Godot 4.8 for the bundled `.scn` resources. `render_one_sheet_animation.py` renders the source frames. `tools/runtime/capture_bedroll_henry_studio.gd` captures the real Henry, pack, inventory pickup and both game states under Forward+/Vulkan against a neutral test floor. The [review video](../../../docs/asset_previews/bedroll/bedroll_game_ready_review.mp4) clearly separates the Blender roll sequence from the Godot runtime shots.
