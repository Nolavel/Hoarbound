HOARBOUND - SMOKE TEST

Windows: unzip the whole archive and run Hoarbound.exe. Keep the data_* folder next to the exe.
Linux: unzip and run ./Hoarbound.x86_64.
A Vulkan-capable graphics card is required. Graphics default to LOW; MEDIUM and HIGH are in the settings.

Keys: F take / act (hold to take into the hand), Tab backpack, 1-4 and the wheel for pockets,
X put away what is in the hands, G drop, C lay out / roll up the bedroll.
In the Tab Hub, an item dragged onto the "Hands" card is in Henry's hand after you close it.

What to check: you start outside 727 Fort Street; go in through the glass door, come out and back,
board the windows, light the stove, eat, sleep and save,
then restart and choose Continue in the menu. Version and commit are in BUILD_INFO.txt.
New in 0.318a: 727 is rebuilt as the real Key West clinic (2020 permit drawings): the entrance
is the glass storefront on the side street, new windows, canopy, sign and snow on the roof;
the neighbouring Lofts and the Douglass Community Center now stand where they really are.

KNOWN: the snow around Henry could change height in a square as he walked. A likely cause is
fixed but not confirmed on hardware - tell us if you see it.

LOG: the logs folder next to the exe. Send the newest hoarbound_*.log with the steps you took
(and a screenshot if something looks wrong).

ROUTE RECORDING: found a bug that repeats? Press F9, repeat the steps, press F9 again.
A tape_*.json file appears in the tapes folder next to the exe - send it with the log:
we will replay exactly your route.

ONE-KEY REPORT: F10 saves a zip to the reports folder next to the exe with the log, settings,
system information, the last route recording and a screenshot. If the game crashed or froze,
such a report is saved by itself on the next start. Attach this zip to your message.
