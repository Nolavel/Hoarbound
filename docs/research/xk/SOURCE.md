## Evidence and uncertainty

The upstream author describes automated YouTube subtitles, speech recognition and AI extraction; a second agent checks claims against transcripts, followed by scripted checks. This process helps traceability but **does not guarantee accuracy**.

References such as `[2026-10-31 · 186 00:12:00]` identify a session and time in the corresponding source. Before asserting a significant design principle as xk's advice, open that session and, when necessary, the video at the indicated timestamp. Watch for comments from guests, conditional advice, outdated context and later corrections in [the revisions chapter](https://github.com/Dis1re/xk_streamparser/blob/main/notes/99_устаревшие_советы.md).

## Usage and rights

The repository is publicly readable but, as of the reviewed revision, **does not declare a reuse license**. Its README also identifies rights holders of the original video material. Public access is not a blanket grant to republish a complete derived database.

Hoarbound therefore stores **only original navigation, attribution and project-specific critical questions**. Do not vendor upstream Markdown, full transcripts or bulk claims into this repository without a suitable license or permission. Use source links, concise attributed references and timestamps for discussion.
