# Hoarbound — Short Pitch

**Hoarbound** is a third-person systemic survival game set in a frozen Key West after a climate catastrophe.

Henry leaves a bunker with little margin for error. Cold, wind, wet clothing, carried weight, fatigue and time work as one pressure system. Finding supplies is only half the problem: the player still has to carry them to shelter, seal the building, keep a fire alive, dry out and make it through the night.

The survival grammar takes clear inspiration from **The Long Dark**: scarcity, preparation, route choice and the constant trade between time, warmth and resources. Hoarbound is not intended as a copy of its mechanics. The project explores that pressure through third-person movement, physical interaction, real-world island geography and shelters that must be made safe rather than simply discovered.

The current production target is deliberately small and readable:

**bunker -> route choice -> scavenging -> worsening weather -> shelter -> repair -> fire -> sleep/save**

If a player can look at the weather, the load they are carrying and the fuel left for the night and naturally start changing their plan, the core idea is working.

Hoarbound is still in active design. Systems, timings, inventory rules, interactions and parts of the loop may be tuned, simplified or replaced when playtesting shows that they do not improve survival decisions.
