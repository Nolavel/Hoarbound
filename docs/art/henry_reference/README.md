# Henry — owner's reference

The owner's canonical look for Henry (2026-11-03). Use it for every change to his body, head and face.

- `henry_body_turnaround.webp` — full body front / 3/4 / side / back, close-ups of face, chest, hand, knee.
- `henry_head_sheet.webp` — head turnaround, wireframe (about 6.2k tris), UV layout, 1024² albedo / normal /
  roughness / metallic, close-ups of eye, ear, nose, lips, hair.

What defines him:

- **Build:** very tall and gaunt, with long thin limbs. The ribs, collarbones and spine show, and the
  knees, elbows and hands are bony with visible veins. Narrow shoulders, slightly hunched.
- **Head:** long and narrow, with a long nose and large, low-set, pointed ears. Deep-set pale green eyes
  with red rims and dark bags. Thin lips and hollow cheeks. Heavy brows with worry lines. Long neck with
  an Adam's apple.
- **Hair:** short, black, messy spiky tufts on top; the sides and back are shaved short.
- **Skin:** pale, greyish-olive with pink ears, nose and eyelids.
- **Rendering:** painterly, faceted brush strokes in the albedo (it matches the game's painterly NPR
  shader), soft normals, mostly rough.
