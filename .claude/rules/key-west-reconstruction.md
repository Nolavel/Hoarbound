# Reconstructing Key West

- **VERIFIED 2026-11-18 (owner):** the goal is a recognisable real Key West, not a plausible American town.
  Missing information is never permission to invent architecture; mark it uncertain and ask.
- **VERIFIED 2026-11-18 (owner):** when official data, drawings and permitted imagery cannot settle a building,
  its facade or position, ask the owner for Google Maps / Street View screenshots.
  - Name the address, the side of the building and the view you need.
  - Screenshots are visual checks only. Sizes and coordinates come from sources that allow extraction.
  - A missing screenshot never blocks the work: continue with confirmed buildings, leave the doubtful ones open.
- **VERIFIED 2026-11-18 (owner):** approved development plans are an accepted temporary geometry source.
  Each such building keeps its record number, date, accuracy and the flag `as_designed_not_surveyed`.
- **VERIFIED 2026-11-18:** checking that every library building reaches the game is not checking that the city
  is complete. The 2017 city footprints, 2019 lidar and January 2023 NAIP all predate Lofts at Bahama Village
  (built 2023–2025, opposite 727); OSM and Overture lacked it too. Look for post-survey construction in
  Legistar planning records (`webapi.legistar.com/v1/keywest/matters`), not only in imagery.
